Profile

My background combines formal quantitative training, production software experience, and a growing body of applied AI work. That experience gave me a strong operational instinct: I like systems that are useful, testable, and resilient under real constraints.

I am now completing George Brown Polytechnic's Applied AI Solutions Development program while pursuing independent research and writing on language models. My interests include model persona, self-description, evaluation design, prompt engineering, fine-tuning workflows, and practical interfaces for human-AI collaboration.
